# 6.6 System Functionalities

## 6.6.1 Login and Authentication

The PMH Inventory Management System implements a secure authentication mechanism that allows administrators and staff to access the system using their unique login credentials. The authentication process begins when users navigate to the login screen, where they are required to enter their registered email address and password. The system validates these credentials against the user database using bcrypt password hashing for enhanced security. Upon successful authentication, the system generates a JSON Web Token (JWT) that serves as a secure session identifier, which is stored on the client side and included in subsequent API requests to maintain the user's authenticated state.

The system supports two distinct user roles: administrators and staff members. Administrators have full access to all system functionalities, including product management, supplier management, purchase records, sales processing, user administration, and comprehensive reporting capabilities. Staff members have restricted access designed for their operational responsibilities, allowing them to view and search products, process sales transactions, view their sales history, generate printable invoices, and access basic dashboard metrics. The JWT token includes role information that the system's authorization middleware checks to ensure that users can only access features appropriate to their assigned role.

The authentication system includes security features such as password hashing using bcrypt, automatic token expiration after 8 hours, and role-based access control that prevents unauthorized access to sensitive administrative functions. Users can securely log out of the system, which clears their JWT token and redirects them to the login screen. The system also provides demo accounts for testing purposes: admin@pmh.lk with password Admin@123 for administrative access, and staff@pmh.lk with password Staff@123 for staff-level access.

## 6.6.2 Dashboard

The dashboard serves as the central information hub of the PMH Inventory Management System, providing administrators and staff with a comprehensive overview of important inventory and business metrics at a glance. The dashboard displays key performance indicators in visually appealing card format, including total product count, today's sales amount with transaction count, low-stock item alerts, and monthly revenue figures. For administrators, the dashboard shows complete system-wide statistics, while staff members see personalized metrics focused on their individual sales performance and basic inventory information.

The dashboard features interactive cards that users can click to navigate directly to relevant sections of the system. For example, clicking on the "Total Products" card takes users to the products page, while the "Today's Sales" card navigates to the sales history. The low-stock alerts section automatically identifies products that have fallen below their reorder levels, enabling proactive inventory management by highlighting items that need to be replenished. This real-time monitoring helps prevent stockouts and ensures that popular spare parts remain available for customers.

The dashboard's data is dynamically retrieved from the backend API, ensuring that the displayed information is always current and reflects the latest inventory levels, sales transactions, and business performance metrics. The visual design uses color-coded indicators and clear typography to make critical information immediately recognizable, with different colors for various metric types (blue for product count, green for sales, amber for low-stock alerts, and royal blue for revenue). This intuitive presentation enables quick decision-making and efficient monitoring of the business's operational health.

## 6.6.3 Product Management

The product management functionality enables administrators to maintain comprehensive information about all vehicle spare parts in the PMH inventory. Administrators can create new product entries by specifying essential details including product name, brand, vehicle type, part type (Genuine, OEM, Aftermarket, or Used), genuine status, cost price, selling price, discount price, current stock quantity, and reorder level. The system supports editing existing product information to update prices, adjust stock levels, modify product details, or correct any data entry errors. Administrators can also delete products that are no longer part of the inventory.

The product management interface includes powerful search and filtering capabilities that allow both administrators and staff to quickly locate specific products. Users can search by product name, SKU, or other identifying criteria, and filter products by category or vehicle type. The system also provides a dedicated low-stock view that displays only products that have fallen below their defined reorder levels, enabling efficient inventory monitoring and replenishment planning. Each product record displays comprehensive information including brand, vehicle type, part type, pricing details, current stock levels, and reorder thresholds.

The product management system is designed with role-based access control, ensuring that only administrators can create, modify, or delete product information. Staff members have read-only access to product data, allowing them to view and search products but preventing unauthorized modifications to inventory information. This separation of responsibilities maintains data integrity and ensures that product information remains accurate and consistent. The system also supports validation of input data to prevent errors and maintain data quality across the product database.

## 6.6.4 Supplier Management

The supplier management functionality provides administrators with the tools to maintain comprehensive information about all suppliers that provide vehicle spare parts to PMH. Administrators can create new supplier records by entering essential details such as supplier name, contact information, address, and other relevant business information. The system allows editing of existing supplier details to update contact information, modify addresses, or add additional business details as supplier relationships evolve. Administrators can also remove suppliers that are no longer active or have been discontinued from the supply chain.

The supplier management interface displays all registered suppliers in a tabular format with clear organization of information. Each supplier record shows the supplier's name, contact details, address, and any other relevant business information that has been entered into the system. This centralized supplier database enables efficient supplier relationship management and provides quick access to supplier contact information when needed for procurement activities or business communications.

Like other administrative functions, supplier management is restricted to administrators only, ensuring that supplier information remains under proper control and preventing unauthorized modifications. This role-based access control maintains the integrity of supplier data and ensures that supplier relationships are managed by authorized personnel who understand the business requirements and supplier agreements. The supplier management system integrates with the purchase management functionality, allowing administrators to select from registered suppliers when recording new purchases, streamlining the procurement process and ensuring accurate supplier assignment to purchase transactions.

## 6.6.5 Purchase Management

The purchase management functionality enables administrators to record purchases from suppliers and automatically update inventory quantities to reflect incoming stock. When administrators create a new purchase record, they specify the supplier, purchase date, and a list of items being purchased. For each purchase item, the system records the product being purchased, the quantity purchased, and the cost price. The purchase management system uses database transactions to ensure data integrity, recording the purchase header information and all associated purchase items as a single atomic operation.

As each purchase item is processed, the system automatically increases the stock quantity of the corresponding product in the inventory database. This automatic stock update ensures that inventory levels remain accurate and current without requiring manual intervention. The transaction-based approach guarantees that either all purchase information is successfully recorded and stock levels updated, or none of the changes are applied, preventing inconsistent inventory states that could occur if the process were interrupted.

The purchase management system maintains a complete history of all purchases, allowing administrators to review past procurement activities, track supplier performance, and analyze purchasing patterns over time. Each purchase record includes detailed information about the supplier, purchase date, all items purchased with quantities and costs, and the total purchase amount. This comprehensive purchase history supports business analysis and helps administrators make informed decisions about supplier relationships, purchasing strategies, and inventory planning.

Purchase management is an administrative-only function, reflecting the business requirement that only authorized personnel should be able to record supplier purchases and update inventory levels. This restriction ensures that inventory updates are properly controlled and that supplier relationships are managed by authorized administrators who understand the business requirements and procurement processes.

## 6.6.6 Sales Management

The sales management functionality enables both administrators and staff to process customer sales transactions efficiently and accurately. When processing a sale, users specify the sale date and add multiple items to the transaction. For each sale item, the system records the product being sold, the quantity sold, and the selling price. The sales management system implements row-level locking using database transactions to ensure data integrity during the sales process, preventing conflicts when multiple users attempt to process sales simultaneously.

Before completing a sale, the system validates that sufficient stock is available for each item being sold. This stock validation prevents overselling and ensures that the system never allows inventory quantities to fall below zero. If sufficient stock is available, the system records the sale, creates sale item records, and automatically decrements the stock quantity for each product in the inventory database. If insufficient stock is available for any item, the system rolls back the entire transaction and displays an error message, preventing the sale from completing and maintaining inventory accuracy.

The sales management system generates printable invoices and receipts for completed sales, providing customers with detailed transaction records. Each invoice includes the sale date, customer information (if recorded), all items sold with quantities and prices, subtotal calculations, and the total amount due. The invoice generation functionality supports both digital display and printable formats, enabling customers to receive immediate confirmation of their purchase.

The system maintains a complete sales history that can be viewed by administrators and staff. Administrators can view all sales transactions across the system, while staff members can view only their own sales history, supporting individual performance tracking and accountability. The sales history includes detailed information about each sale, including the sale date, the user who processed the sale, all items sold, and the total amount. This comprehensive sales data supports business analysis, revenue tracking, and performance evaluation.

## 6.6.7 Inventory Management

The inventory management functionality provides continuous monitoring and maintenance of product stock information throughout the PMH Inventory Management System. The system automatically tracks stock quantities for all products, updating these quantities in real-time as sales are processed and purchases are recorded. This automated stock tracking eliminates the need for manual inventory reconciliation and ensures that inventory levels remain accurate and current across all system operations.

The system implements reorder level monitoring to identify products that require replenishment. Each product record includes a reorder level threshold that defines the minimum stock quantity that should be maintained. When a product's stock quantity falls below this threshold, the system automatically flags it as low-stock and displays it prominently in the low-stock alerts section of the dashboard. This proactive monitoring enables administrators to replenish stock before complete stockouts occur, ensuring that popular spare parts remain available for customers.

The inventory management system provides comprehensive visibility into stock levels across all products. Users can view current stock quantities, compare them against reorder levels, and identify products that are approaching critical stock levels. The system supports filtering and searching specifically for low-stock items, enabling efficient inventory monitoring and prioritization of replenishment activities. This visibility supports effective inventory planning and helps prevent revenue loss due to stockouts.

The inventory management functionality is integrated with both sales and purchase management systems. When sales are processed, the system automatically decrements stock quantities, and when purchases are recorded, the system automatically increments stock quantities. This integration ensures that inventory levels remain synchronized with actual business activities, providing a single source of truth for stock information across the organization.

## 6.6.8 User Management

The user management functionality enables administrators to manage system user accounts and control access permissions within the PMH Inventory Management System. Administrators can create new user accounts by specifying the user's name, email address, password, and role (admin or staff). The system uses bcrypt password hashing to securely store passwords, ensuring that sensitive credential information is protected even in the event of database compromise. The user creation process includes validation to prevent duplicate email addresses, ensuring that each user account has a unique identifier.

Administrators can view all user accounts in the system, displaying each user's name, email address, and assigned role. This user listing provides administrators with visibility into who has access to the system and what permissions each user possesses. The system allows administrators to delete user accounts when personnel leave the organization or when access needs to be revoked. To prevent accidental system lockout, the system includes a self-deletion prevention mechanism that prevents administrators from deleting their own accounts.

The user management system implements role-based access control that determines what functionalities each user can access. Administrators have full access to all system features, including product management, supplier management, purchase recording, sales processing, user administration, and comprehensive reporting. Staff members have restricted access designed for their operational responsibilities, including product viewing, sales processing, sales history viewing, invoice generation, and basic dashboard access. This role-based security ensures that users can only access features appropriate to their job responsibilities.

User management is an administrative-only function, reflecting the business requirement that only authorized administrators should be able to manage user accounts and access permissions. This restriction maintains security and ensures that system access is properly controlled by authorized personnel who understand the organizational structure and access requirements.

## 6.6.9 Report Generation

The report generation functionality provides administrators with comprehensive reporting capabilities to support inventory monitoring, business analysis, and decision-making. The system can generate multiple types of reports including daily sales reports, monthly revenue reports, and profit analysis reports. These reports aggregate data from sales transactions, inventory records, and purchase history to provide actionable insights into business performance and inventory trends.

The daily sales report shows sales transactions for a specified date range, including detailed information about each sale such as the sale date, products sold, quantities, prices, and total amounts. This report enables administrators to analyze daily sales patterns, identify peak sales periods, and track sales performance over time. The monthly revenue report aggregates sales data by month, showing revenue trends and enabling comparison of performance across different time periods. This supports seasonal analysis and helps identify business cycles.

The profit analysis report provides insights into the profitability of sales transactions by comparing selling prices against cost prices. This report enables administrators to understand profit margins on different products, identify the most profitable items, and make informed decisions about pricing strategies and product focus. The report can be filtered by date ranges to analyze profitability trends over time.

The report generation functionality is restricted to administrators only, reflecting the business requirement that detailed business analysis and reporting capabilities should be available to authorized management personnel. The reports are generated dynamically from current system data, ensuring that the information is always accurate and up-to-date. The reports can be viewed directly in the system interface and support filtering by date ranges and other criteria to enable targeted analysis of specific time periods or business segments.
